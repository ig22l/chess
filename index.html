Offline, all the way down.

Genuine Stockfish 19, full NNUE, one CPU thread. Its full WebAssembly engine and neural network are bundled in the local assets folder, split into six small script files for easy upload. MultiPV shows up to three legal candidate moves. Scores always describe White's position. Analysis never makes a move for you.

Chess rules and notation: chess.js 1.4.0. Pieces: original inline SVG artwork. Local Blob workers keep analysis off the interface thread, with a cooperative main-thread engine fallback. All scripts and styles load from the same folder. No internet resources are used; fetch and other data connections are disabled by the app's content security policy.

Threefold repetition and the 50-move rule allow a draw claim. Fivefold repetition, the 75-move rule, stalemate, and insufficient mating material end the game automatically. Checkmate takes precedence. Your game stays in this tab; closing or reloading it clears the game.
